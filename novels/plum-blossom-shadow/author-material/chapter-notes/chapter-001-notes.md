# Author notes after Chapter 1: The Honest Blade

**TECHNIQUES REFERENCED IN THIS CHAPTER**

*The following entries are provided for reference. They were not explained during the chapter because they did not need to be. They are recorded here for completeness.*

**Honest Blade — First Form: Zhǐ Huā — Guiding Bloom**   *指花  ·  Zhǐ Huā*

*[ Offensive — Constellation of the Ten Legendary Swords, First Aspect ]*

Three successive strikes targeting the opponent's meridian junctions or pressure points in sequence. The goal is disruption of Qi flow, not raw damage. When executed against a fully enhanced opponent, the strikes follow the Qi circuit the opponent has activated — their own power becomes a map of where to hit. Land all three and the technique the opponent is building collapses from the inside. The final strike carries physical force as well as disruption; the combination drops the opponent rather than simply staggering them. To most observers, the sequence reads as a single committed strike. The speed required to land all three within one motion is the primary reason the technique is difficult to read at this level.

**Third Meridian Resistance**   *—  ·  —*

*[ Personal — Chu Meiying, ongoing ]*

A persistent catch point in Meiying's Qi circulation at the third meridian junction, present since age twelve. Her warm Qi — a characteristic of the Ten Legendary Swords constellation — pools at this point rather than continuing forward on the first pass. She has identified a workaround: reduced pressure with increased directional intent allows the Qi to thin and slide through. The permanent correction has not yet been found. This is her current primary technical problem and the reason she requires three passes to complete a full cycle where most students at her level require one.
