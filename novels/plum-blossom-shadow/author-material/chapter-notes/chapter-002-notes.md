**Elder Shen Cailan**   *沈彩兰*

*[ Character — Instructor ]*

Theory and application instructor for Applied Burdens and Conditional Qi. Iron Vow branch. Fifties. Teaches by discussion rather than lecture — the circular classroom arrangement is her choice. Has carried a Burden herself for an unspecified period. Does not mention this in class. Treats skepticism as a valid starting position and a useful analytical tool. Engaged with Suyin as an equal during the Chapter Two discussion — a distinction she does not make casually.

**Suyin's Sister**   *—*

*[ Open Thread ]*

Wei Suyin's older sister took a Burden three years before Chapter Two. Suyin describes her as managing it well — implying it required management, implying the early period was not smooth. Suyin has spent three years reading everything available on the Burden system because her sister walked into it without enough information. This is the origin of Suyin's depth of knowledge and the reason Burden theory is the only subject that makes her talkative. The sister has not been named. Her Burden has not been described.
