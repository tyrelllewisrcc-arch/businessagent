# Author notes after Chapter 3: Further Than Intended

**NOTES AND OPEN THREADS — CHAPTER THREE**

*The following were not explained during the chapter because they did not need to be. They are recorded here for continuity.*

**Branch Trim Colors — Jianyun Sect**   *—*

*[ World Detail — Established ]*

All Jianyun Sect robes are ink-black. Branch identity is indicated by colored trim. Student level: a single narrow line at collar and cuff. Senior disciples: broader trim at collar, cuff, and hem. Elders: full color panels at shoulder and sleeve. The eight branch colors are as follows — Chu House (Celestial Sword): gold. Iron Sash (Binding Sash): silver-grey. Flame Herald (Flame Spear): burnt orange. Still Water (Quenching Fan): pale jade. Iron Vow (Crescent Blade): deep crimson. Shifting Wind (Infinite Staff): storm blue. Dragon Crown (Nine Dragons): imperial purple. Still Blade (Ten Legendary Swords): white.

**Silence Domain — Unidentified Technique**   *—*

*[ Technique — Unidentified, Unnamed ]*

The complete absence of sound observed in the hollow during the meeting. Not natural quiet — the silence had edges, a quality of something imposed rather than absent. The birds on that section of slope were not audible. The two figures were clearly speaking but produced no sound that reached the ridge above. At Body Tempering level, Meiying and her companions lack the cultivation or theoretical framework to identify this as a technique. They know it was wrong. They do not know what it was. Name and practitioner to be established when the thread becomes relevant.

**The Foreign Emblem**   *—*

*[ Open Thread — Critical ]*

A geometric symbol worn at the collar of the unidentified figure — interlocking angular lines forming a closed, deliberate shape. Not a Jianyun Sect emblem. Not recognizable to three Body Tempering students. Suyin believes she has encountered it in her reading — not yet placed. The emblem's origin will tell the reader what kind of meeting they stumbled onto. The Iron Sash branch maintains a catalogue of external political symbols. Meiying has identified this as the likely source but approaching them directly would be visible.

**The Shifting Wind Elder**   *—*

*[ Open Thread ]*

An unidentified elder from the Shifting Wind branch conducting an unscheduled meeting on the outer slopes with a foreign figure. Broad storm-blue panels indicating senior rank. Not recognized by Chengqi, who knows his branch's senior members well. The elder's identity and the purpose of the meeting are unknown. This thread connects to the compendium note that the Shifting Wind branch's creative chaos masks structural problems nobody has addressed.

**Chengqi — Branch Loyalty**   *—*

*[ Character Thread ]*

Chapter Three established that Chengqi's relationship with his branch is personal enough that seeing its emblem in an irregular context produces genuine distress. He does not want the meeting to be what it looks like. The comedy left him entirely and did not fully return. His loyalty to his branch and his loyalty to what is right may eventually be in tension. This should be tracked.
