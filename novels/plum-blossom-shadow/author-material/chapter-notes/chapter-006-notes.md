# Author notes after Chapter 6: Second

**NOTES AND OPEN THREADS — CHAPTER SIX**

*The following were not explained during the chapter because they did not need to be. They are recorded here for continuity.*

**Han Jingwei**   *韩敬威 — Respectful Power*

*[ Character — Introduced ]*

Iron Vow branch. Crescent Blade constellation. Age fourteen to fifteen. The only student in the combat class who watched where Meiying's eyes went before she moved during the Wen Jiabao spar — Chapter One. His first direct interaction with Meiying is a sparring match he loses narrowly. He does not acknowledge her as anything other than the Sect Head's daughter — a categorization, not a greeting, delivered as he walks out the door. His relationship with Meiying is rivalry without friendship. Both are intelligent. Both are unwilling to be second. The tension between them is not hostility — it is the specific friction of two people who recognize each other and do not entirely like what they see. He will be a recurring presence. He will not become friendly.

**Elder Deng Suhua**   *邓素华*

*[ Character — Introduced ]*

Senior instructor, Flame Herald branch. Broad burnt-orange trim — senior elder rank. Combat theory instructor replacing Elder Fang's suspended classes. Teaching style: pressure-based, direct, no patience for hesitation or sentimentality. She stopped the Meiying-Han Jingwei spar when the jian reached Han Jingwei's throat — her assessment: dead, then barely, directed at Han Jingwei and Meiying respectively. She does not explain her assignments or her assessments. She creates situations and watches what you do in them. The announcement of Elder Fang's death was delivered mid-class without softening and the class resumed immediately — deliberate or simply her nature. Possibly both.

**Han Jingwei — The Sect Head's Daughter**   *—*

*[ Character Note — Relationship Dynamic ]*

Han Jingwei's only words to Meiying after the spar: the Sect Head's daughter. Not her name. Not acknowledgment of the fight's outcome. A categorization — the only thing he has decided she is. This is the baseline of their relationship at Chapter Six. He does not see her as a cultivator or a rival yet. He sees her as a title. This will change. The rate at which it changes and what forces the change is an open thread.

**Suyin — Found Something**   *—*

*[ Open Thread — Critical ]*

Suyin found something in her emblem research last night but was not focused enough during her spar to confirm its significance. She states: I found something. I need to look at it again before I say what it is. Tonight I will know if it is the right thing. This is Suyin's most careful phrasing to date — distinguishing between finding something and finding the right thing. The emblem thread will resolve in Chapter Seven or produce a complication that reframes the investigation.

**Elder Fang — Formal Announcement**   *—*

*[ Event — Closed ]*

The sect formally announced Elder Fang's passing to classes via Iron Sash senior disciple. The announcement was delivered mid-combat-class by Elder Deng Suhua without embellishment: Elder Fang passed two days ago, his position will be filled permanently within the month, his classes are suspended until then. No cause of death stated in the announcement. The official handling is contained and clinical. What the Iron Sash investigation has determined internally is not part of the public announcement and remains unknown to Meiying.
