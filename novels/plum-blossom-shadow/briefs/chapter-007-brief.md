# Chapter 7 Brief: The Right Thing

**Conflicts with author's instructions:** None. The author's standing direction (more action and visible growth, slow reveals) is built in: the chapter is a rooftop chase with a named growth step, and the seal finding is the only reveal.

**Chapter conventions (match Ch 1 to 6):**
- Heading: `# Chapter 7: The Right Thing`
- A short italic epigraph in broken lines, then `—`, then an italic in-world attribution. Suggested (the lead writer may replace it): *A search that takes the wrong thing / tells you what the searcher was told to find. / A search that takes nothing / tells you what he was told to fear.* — *Iron Sash junior disciples' primer, "On Rooms"*
- Scene breaks marked `―――`
- Last line: `*— End of Chapter Seven —*`
- Target 2,500 to 3,500 words (aim about 3,200; never over 4,000).

## Purpose
Delivers the "tonight I will know" payoff (the emblem is a binding seal, not a crest) inside a night chase, so the chapter is the first on-page test of the secondary channel's three-sequence limit under pressure. It also tells Meiying and the reader that someone is already hunting the trio's secret, and settles her one-day deadline: she will go to Rou, and Rou moves first.

## Starts at
Ch 6 ends at midday on Day 1. The three of them are walking away from the combat class hall into the outer sect. Suyin has just said "Tonight I will know if it is the right thing." Meiying has just thought "The question was whether ready would be enough." Her bruised forearm and shoulder are making themselves known. Open with the walk still in progress, then move quickly to dusk.

## POV, setting, characters present
- **POV:** Chu Meiying only, close third, past tense.
- **Setting:** Day 1. Midday on the path out of the combat hall, the afternoon in the indoor training hall, then dusk (lanterns being lit as the mountain "steals the sun") in and above the Iron Vow residential building and the lanes down to the outer wall.
- **Present:** Meiying, Luo Chengqi, Wei Suyin (she arrives late, after the chase), the hooded intruder (Tan Ji; unnamed, face never seen), Qin Mu (final scene; Meiying recognises him as the Iron Sash courier from Ch 1 but does not know his name until he gives it).

## Scenes

### Scene 1: Quarter-Breath (about 600 words)
- **Goal:** Get to Suyin's finding tonight, and decide whom to tell and how much.
- **Conflict:** Suyin cannot be at her room until after an Iron Vow evening round. Meiying is on a clock she set herself ("I'll tell you tomorrow") and is alone with the weight of what she does not know.
- **Key beats:**
  - Pick up on the walk. Suyin says she is held for an Iron Vow evening endurance round (a branch senior assigned it to everyone who lost a bout today; she does not complain, which is Iron Vow). Meetup at her room after it, at dusk. Chengqi has a Shifting Wind branch errand. Meiying: "Tonight. I'll tell you then." She means whom she picks and why.
  - Afternoon in the indoor hall, kept short. She tests the channel again. **The initiation delay is now a quarter-breath** (half a breath on Ch 5, shorter each day). State it plainly, with the way she measures it. She runs the sustained test and feels it begin to thin after three sequences, same as before. Her sister Wanru's line comes to her here or in Scene 2 ("Qi is a conversation"). One clause must call Wanru her sister outright (bible reveal rule: early Arc 1, on the page).
  - Dusk. Meiying and Chengqi meet and walk to the Iron Vow building together. Chengqi is quieter than he was this morning, still holding the name. Short, clipped exchange: what happens if the finding is the right one? Meiying: it changes who we tell and how much.
  - Most of the building is at the evening meal or the round. Suyin's door on the upper floor is ajar. Suyin never leaves it ajar. Meiying pushes it with two fingers.
  - A figure in a plain black hood is at Suyin's shelves, hands in the books. Plain black robes, **no trim at collar or cuff** (Meiying sees this at once). Lower face only: a young jaw, nothing more. He freezes, sees the two of them in the doorway, and bolts for the east window rather than past them.
- **Outcome:** The chase begins. Meiying has seen "no trim" and a young jaw and nothing else.

### Scene 2: Over the Roofs (about 1,100 words)
- **Goal:** Catch him, or at least see his face.
- **Conflict:** He is a Body Tempering Late runner (older, heavier-built, running on fear). She is Body Tempering Peak with a bruised forearm and shoulder, unarmed (her jian is at the Chu residence), on damp slate at dusk, with a channel that thins after three sequences.
- **Key beats:**
  - The window opens onto the tiled roof of a lower wing. The runner crosses it, then the next roof, then drops to a service stair. Meiying opens the secondary channel in a quarter-breath (the new delay, proved under pressure for the first time) and goes after him. She reads him as she runs: he is terrified (frightened people run for what they know and take the nearest exit), his legs shimmer faintly at the knee (visible Qi, step 1 perception), he turns right at every fork.
  - Chengqi shouts what he sees: "He goes right every time. Always the lower route." Shifting Wind disciples cut roofs to skip the stair queues, so he knows these lines. He **declines to follow behind** and takes a different roofline instead (the Ch 6 method: find the rhythm, decline to be where they expect, then be there). He drops into the runner's path at the one fork where the runner would turn right. The runner stops dead, reverses, loses several paces and takes the service stair down.
  - Sequence count on the page: one for the first roof sprint, two for the drop and the stair, three when she closes on him on the stair and grabs his sleeve. Coarse cloth, no trim, and a smell of fresh-cut pine pitch that she cannot place yet (plant for the new gatehouse timber, Ch 3; she does not connect it).
  - She tries Guiding Bloom unarmed at the grab (it works unarmed, bible rule). The channel **thins on the third sequence**: Qi arrives late and ragged, the strike lands soft. It registers (his arm stumbles, he is Body Tempering Late with a higher threshold, as Han was) but not enough to hold him. He tears free.
  - He reaches the outer-wall steps first and vanishes down into the lantern-lit lanes of the outer sect among market crowds. Meiying stops at the top of the steps, breathing hard, palm scraped on slate. She knows the limit is real because she felt it narrow.
- **Outcome:** He gets away. Cost: Meiying's Ch 6 bruises hurt more, her palm is scraped, and she has learned what three sequences feels like when it matters. Gain: the channel opened at once, the delay really is a quarter-breath, and Chengqi has proved the trio's method works. No face, but two physical details: no trim, pine pitch.

### Scene 3: The Right Thing (about 1,100 words)
- **Goal:** Find out what he was after and get Suyin's finding on the table. Decide who to tell and what to say.
- **Conflict:** The three want different things: Meiying wants Rou now, Suyin wants to hold the finding back, Chengqi will not say his name even now.
- **Key beats:**
  - Meiying and Chengqi walk back up. Suyin is standing in her own doorway with her satchel, still sweating from the round, looking at the room. Deadpan. Her first act is to check one bundle (her sister Jinglan's letters, tied in grey cord): untouched. Then the shelves. She reads the room in seconds: "He turned the stones. He took the herb ledger." (Her system looks random and is not; he searched like someone who believed everything was hidden. Her real material is kept in the dullest places she owns.) The herb ledger has Day -5's counts and the route down the slope; no note of what they saw, because they agreed to tell no one. Humour from character, not from undercutting.
  - What he wanted: "what she wrote." Suyin writes things down. Her emblem sketch and last night's notes are in the small notebook she carries on her person. The text itself (a brittle fragment of old contract writing from the east, about a dozen leaves, bought from the hand-copied-texts stall two years ago; the writer picks the title) is on the shelf in a plain wrapper marked like a grain ledger. He passed it twice.
  - **The finding, one beat at a time.** Suyin lays out the plate: interlocking angular lines forming a closed shape. Meiying confirms the match from what only she noticed: the third stroke from the bottom stops a hair short of the corner. "Neither piece alone": Suyin's text plus Meiying's eye. Suyin: "It's the right thing." What the text says, in short plain sentences:
    1. Closed figures of this kind are **binding seals**, not crests: they close a bargain, they do not name a house.
    2. They are struck **in pairs**; the second is called only "the answer" in the text, and it is not described. (Do NOT describe it as "open"; do NOT mention bound spaces or silence. See Plant.)
    3. The closed figure marks a **keeper**, who is bound (a custodial binding) to a **line** the seal must not cross.
    4. Buried in a list of how paired seals behave, in one short clause: they "answer each other across a few li" and the bearer's cloth takes the mark. Nobody remarks on it.
  - If someone asks where the answer is held, Suyin says the text does not say. Nobody links the keeper to Fang yet. A line of Meiying's mind may flick toward "bound to a line, and a man who died of a broken condition" and be cut off by the argument; she has no pair on the mountain to attach it to. The link is Ch 12 and 13.
  - They work out the intruder's likely origin with Chengqi's callback: plain black, no trim, outer disciples. Who saw the three of them go out and come back? "Good haul?" They decide the intruder may be someone from the gate. It is only a guess; hood, no face. (The Iron Sash reveal that the gatekeeper was not posted by them is Ch 8.)
  - **The argument.** Meiying wants Rou now: someone has already searched a room. Suyin: Rou is either ignorant or complicit, depending on whether Fang's Burden was in the Iron Sash records (her Ch 5 fork), and the finding is the only thing they hold that the Iron Sash may not. If the closed figure is not in the Iron Sash catalogue, Rou will not know what it is; if it is, she has said nothing. Telling Rou the finding costs the leverage. Compromise: tell Rou about the meeting and about tonight's intruder; hold the finding back; Meiying watches how Rou's body takes what she is told. Chengqi: asked about the name, "If I say it tonight it can't be wrong anymore, and I don't want it not wrong." He stays silent. Suyin watches him and says nothing.
  - Meiying agrees, and feels it: the Honest Blade is "nothing added, nothing withheld". She is withholding. It is the first time she has chosen to. Show it through her hand running the guard position, not a speech.
- **Outcome:** Suyin's "tonight I will know" is paid off. Plan agreed: Meiying will ask to see Rou tomorrow, tell her about the meeting and the intruder, and keep the finding and Chengqi's name back. Meiying now carries a withheld thing for the first time.

### Scene 4: The Eave (about 350 words)
- **Goal:** Shut the window and go home.
- **Conflict:** Someone has been there the whole time.
- **Key beats:**
  - Before they leave, Chengqi wedges the east window shut (it has no bar; the intruder left it open and it could be opened from the roof). Suyin has already latched the shutter before the finding, so nothing said in the room could have been overheard (state this plainly so Qin Mu's silence about what he heard stays honest).
  - Meiying steps into the lane under the eave to start home. A figure sits on the roof ridge above, a silhouette against the lanterns: broad silver-grey Iron Sash trim, a sealed case under one arm. She knows him before he moves: the courier from the morning of the spar. He drops lightly to the eave's edge. "Qin Mu. Iron Sash." He says his orders were to watch, not to catch. She asks him whom he watched: "Yes." (The answer is both the runner and the three of them.)
  - His message, nothing more: "Head Rou will see Chu Meiying at first light. Wei Suyin should bar her window." He is gone over the roof.
  - Close on Meiying alone for a beat: she had planned to ask for an audience tomorrow, and Rou has already asked first. The runner searched Suyin's room. Qin Mu watched him do it. Someone has been watching all of them for longer than a night.
- **Outcome:** Deadline overtaken. Meiying must go to Rou at first light, alone (the message names only her), carrying the one thing she has chosen to withhold.
