## Overall story

- **Where it starts:** Chu Meiying, 14, youngest child of Sect Head Chu Wulong, is at the peak of Body Tempering in the Jianyun (Sword Cloud) Sect, weeks from her first breakthrough. She walks the Ten Legendary Swords, the hardest constellation in the sect's path, and has one aspect of ten (the Honest Blade). With Suyin and Chengqi she has witnessed a secret meeting between a Shifting Wind elder and a foreign envoy wearing an eastern emblem. Her teacher Elder Fang has since died of Burden backlash.
- **Main conflict (author's premise):** three young disciples quietly investigate a conspiracy inside their own sect and risk the attention of people far more powerful than themselves, while Meiying grows from an ordinary disciple into a powerful cultivator and finds her way of perceiving Qi is far more unusual than she realises. Underneath it runs the theme question: is strength only what you make alone, or also what you are willing to carry and who you let help you?
- **Shape of the whole book (per the bible):**
  1. *What Fang Carried* (Arc 1): who killed Fang through his own Burden, and what the sect has been hiding for 112 years.
  2. *The Whispering Edge* (Arc 2): who the second hand really is, the first Burden decision, the family's cost, the One Call.
  3. *The Eastern Road* (Arc 3): the two-month journey to Qiongling, the other side of the Accord, Foundation Establishment.
  4. *The cost of the tenth aspect* (later arcs).
- **Where it ends:** deliberately open. Fixed: the tenth aspect bears her name and is "what you become when you have mastered all nine and survived the cost of doing so".
- **Tone:** serious and epic, with dry character humour that never undercuts a fight or a death. No romance planned.
- **Length and pace:** Arc 1 is 20 chapters at 2,500 to 3,500 words (never over 4,000), about 55,000 to 70,000 words. Royal Road: backlog of 10 to 15 chapters, then 3 a week.

## Arc 1: What Fang Carried (Chapters 1 to 20)

**The question the reader wants answered:** What was Elder Fang carrying, who broke it, and what were a Shifting Wind elder and a stranger from the east doing in the silence?

### Arc card

- **Arc goal:** Find out how and why Elder Fang died and stop it happening again, before the vacant post is filled (about Day 21, "within the month", Ch 6). The post carries custody of the sect's half of the Accord seal, so the appointment is the arc's clock. A personal goal runs alongside: break through to Qi Gathering and learn to fight with Qi without losing the Honest Blade.
- **Main antagonist or obstacle:**
  - *The second hand* (Shen Wuque; not named on the page in Arc 1): a brilliant, unreadable cultivator bound to a breakaway Qiongling house, who wants the renewal to fail in a way that blames the Iron Sash. On the page he is only "a figure whose Qi announces nothing", seen across a training ground (Ch 14) and on the rim of the hollow (Ch 18 and 19).
  - *The watcher* Tan Ji: an outer disciple, plain black robes, no trim, posted to the outer gate under a posting seal the Iron Sash duty roster never issued. He is the second hand's eyes: frightened, not a killer. He is the intruder in Ch 7 and is caught in Ch 16.
  - *Authority and secrecy:* Rou Shenglan knows more than she says and will not share it; Chu Wulong knew of the hollow meeting from Rou's warning on Day -5, has known since Day 0 that the half is missing, and has said nothing to his daughter. He does not know she saw the meeting.
  - *Meiying herself:* her honesty cannot easily hold a secret, and her new Qi pulls against the Honest Blade's rule of nothing added, nothing withheld.
- **Power at the start (end of Ch 6; chapter log):**
  - Realm: Body Tempering, Peak; breakthrough pressure "patient and close".
  - Aspects: 1 of 10 (Honest Blade).
  - Forms: Guiding Bloom **Mastered**; Parting Reed **Can Perform**; Rooted Shore **Can Perform**; Still Water Cuts, Returning Bloom, The Straight Cut, Formless Path **Not Yet**.
  - Endurance: 3 sequences through the secondary channel; half-breath initiation delay, shrinking daily.
  - Perception: step 1 (bodies, habits, visible Qi). Burdens: none.
- **Power at the end (end of Ch 20):**
  - Realm: **Qi Gathering, Early** (breakthrough in Ch 10).
  - Aspects: 1 of 10 unlocked, plus **one glimpse of the second aspect, the Whispering Edge** (Ch 19), named by Shen Qiuyue (Ch 20). It unlocks early in Arc 2, once Still Water Cuts is Reliable.
  - Forms: Guiding Bloom Mastered; **Parting Reed Reliable** (Ch 11); **Rooted Shore Reliable** (Ch 19; breaks on the fourth strike); **Still Water Cuts Can Perform** (Ch 17); Returning Bloom, The Straight Cut, Formless Path Not Yet.
  - Endurance: **7 sequences** (reservoir plus channel).
  - Qi sense: about 10 paces. Restraint: can hold her Qi under the surface through one full Honest Blade sequence. Blade infusion: reinforced, warm edge (Early). Qi step: one short, costly burst of about 4 paces.
  - Perception: steps 1 to 3 (bodies; the edge of an imposed Qi structure; the weight of a Burden in a person). Step 4 (the shape of a condition) belongs to Arc 2.
  - Burdens: none carried. Her first honest answer to "what would I carry one for" is her friends (Ch 10). Fang's posthumous letter names her as his choice of next custodian (Ch 20); it is a nomination, not an offer.
- **Midpoint turn (Ch 14, "Six Years"):** The name Chengqi finally said (Elder Mo Qiyuan) belongs to a man the Shifting Wind roll has listed as "away on a long contract for six years", yet Meiying saw him in the hollow. Someone on the mountain has been quietly collecting his stipend every quarter, and the signature is **Fang Ruochen**, the archivist who has helped her for three years. The investigation changes from "who is the traitor?" to "what is the sect's own secret, and who is turning it?"
- **Climax (Ch 18 and 19, "The Seam" and "Rooted"):** At the new moon (Day 15) the renewal happens in the hollow inside the sealed hearing, a shared Burden whose condition is that no one outside may hear. Rou's plan was to keep every listener away and post Meiying as an edge-watcher. A forged Iron Sash order (carrying a real posting seal) sends Rou's own listening team to the north rim, and the second hand arranges for Rou and the Sect Head to be drawn away. A forced seam is struck in the edge and a cadet's listening thread goes through it. If a word is heard, Mo and Hesu die as Fang did, and the Iron Sash takes the blame. Meiying, who can feel the edge, holds the seam shut with Rooted Shore. It holds three strikes and breaks on the fourth; on the break she has the **glimpse of the Whispering Edge**, a single cut faster than anyone can process, severing the listening thread at the seam. The envoy and the elder live. The half is not recovered; the unreadable figure leaves with a salute.
- **Arc end hook (Ch 20):** Qin Mu, the courier who glanced at her in Ch 1, brings a sealed letter case to her bedside. It is Fang's posthumous letter.

### How the plot works (settled; for the writers, not the reader)

All of this matches the bible's "Truth under the mystery", "The Accord of the Closed Figure" and "How Fang died". The reveals arrive in stages across the table below.

- **The sect's secret.** 112 years ago the sect and the Qiongling houses sealed the Accord of the Closed Figure in the hollow. The archive's "meeting never granted" record is a deliberate false filing (kept in place by Fang Ruochen). The seal is a split disc: highlanders keep the closed half (Hesu's collar emblem), the sect keeps the open half (the 80-year margin drawing). Renewal every seven years at a new moon, in the hollow, by two envoys speaking in the sealed hearing with the halves joined. The sixteenth renewal is due about Day 15.
- **Fang and Mo.** Fang Shouyi was the custodian, bound by an External (custodial) Burden to keep the sect's half inside the boundary stones. Elder Mo Qiyuan is the sect's secret envoy; Hesu is Qiongling's. Hesu came about three weeks early to warn Mo that a breakaway house was moving.
- **Day -5 (Ch 1 to 3).** Dawn: the two halves stir as Hesu nears; Fang's sleeve is stained with binding ink and he cuts it short (Ch 1 already shows a dark ink mark at the cut edge). **This is before the letter.** Before second class Qin Mu delivers Rou's warning. Rou sends the same warning to Chu Wulong, so he knows of the hollow meeting from that day (he does not know his daughter saw it). Afternoon: the trio see Mo and Hesu in the sealed hearing. That night Fang carries the half out to an old gate-shrine niche **inside** the stones; Wen Jiahao sees him go; Tan Ji reports it.
- **Night of Day -1.** The second hand takes the half from the niche and carries it past the stones. Fang's condition breaks and he dies at his desk. The letter did **not** kill him; Meiying's "the backlash would have been immediate" (Ch 5) is wrong, and that error is a fair-play clue.
- **Day 0 (Ch 5).** The Iron Sash reports Fang's death to Chu Wulong. From that morning both he and Rou know the half is missing and hunt it quietly. Rou suspects a leak near the Chu household (the posting seal Lifen lent; not named in Arc 1), which is her real reason for "don't tell your father until I say".
- **What the second hand wants.** The renewal to fail so badly that the highlanders blame the Iron Sash. A lapse by an absent half is the sect's fault; a murdered envoy is the Iron Sash's. He holds the half already.
- **What the sect owes on the page.** The Accord is the sect's most valuable secret; the One Call (term 2) stays unrevealed until Arc 2.

### Fair-play rules for this arc
- Meiying's POV only. She is sometimes wrong (Ch 5's "immediate" theory). Wrong guesses are clues, not cheats.
- Every solution is checked against Burden rules already on the page: the break triggers backlash, not age; External Burdens fail most often; conditions cannot be renegotiated; severity scales with depth; the Iron Sash keeps records of senior members' Burdens.
- **Clues are found by doing:** following, searching, sensing, fighting, getting caught. Every investigation chapter pairs a clue with a progression beat. Talk scenes are short and carry conflict; each person wants something different.
- No "genius". Each big step needs a piece from a friend or relative: "neither piece alone".
- Do not name Shen Wuque or Chu Lifen on the page in Arc 1. Plant only what a careful reader could weigh (see the tracker).
- The reader gets exactly what Meiying gets, when she gets it. No cutaways.
