Review only; nothing in the chapters was changed.

## Editor: must fix

1. **Chapter 1:** say what "Eighteen days" counts down to within the first page or two (the Azure Mountain selection). Trim the cultivation mechanics before the boar fight so the reader reaches the action sooner.
2. **Chapter 2:** cut it by about a quarter.
   - About 15 people are named, most only once; keep the two or three that matter.
   - "I know." appears about nine times.
   - The "eighteen / five" ending repeats three times.
3. **Chapters 2, 3 and 4:** these are three "request refused" chapters in a row, and the "the clan prices me" theme is stated about six times. Give each chapter one small win or discovery, and cut the weaker repetitions of the theme.
4. **Weak endings in Chapters 4 and 5:**
   - Ch 4: "The decision stood. Registration began at first bell."
   - Ch 5: "At the West Garrison he saw what made strength obey."
   - End on something that opens a door instead.
5. **The title promises conquest and power, but the story so far shows a careful survivor.** By Chapter 5, give one flash of something larger: an odd property of Jian's qi, his ambition, or the war's real shape. The enemy and the war are still faceless.
6. **Repeated tics:**
   - jaws tightening and unclenching
   - Rui flinching
   - the "hot wire / white light" leg pain
   - "priced" and "arithmetic"
   - "something shifted"
   - fragment pairs
   - single-line emphasis paragraphs

## Editor: should fix

- **Dialogue:** many characters (Guowei, Lihua, Ge, Qiao, Cao) speak in polished maxims. Let some be blunt, petty or wrong.
- **"As you know" exposition:** Yu Fang reciting the exemption classes, and Guowei reading the order aloud.
- **Chapter 5's first half is a queue:** about 2,000 words before the brawl. Cut the double pricing scene down to one.
- **The brawl's trigger** ("Your lot had the gate open before the Liang finished knocking") is a big reveal with no setup. Jian's punch is also a sudden change for him. One earlier hint would fix both.
- **Similar names:** Shen Peng and Peng Shou; Ge, Gao and Guowei; Qinghe and Qiao.

## Continuity: errors

1. **Sleep (Ch 4 vs Ch 5):** Ch 4 says "Jian did not sleep", but Ch 5 says he "had slept two hours". Fix: change Ch 5 to "had not slept".
2. **Rui's age (Ch 4):** Rui is 18 and selection is every two years, so he would be 20 at the next one, not 19. "Same as you" doesn't work either, because Jian is 17. Fix: change the lines to "twenty", or make Rui 17. Also state the age limit for selection once.
3. **Han Zhen's tag (Ch 5):** blue means Sixth or Seventh and Rui is Seventh, so "a layer either side of Rui" doesn't fit. Fix: "near Rui's own layer".

  - Jian's "uncle" in Ch 1 is never named.
